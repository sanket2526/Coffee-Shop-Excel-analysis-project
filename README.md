# Coffee-Shop-Business-Analytics-Excel-Project

## Project Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive data analytics project focused on the sales performance of a coffee shop chain across multiple locations. Using a dataset sourced from Maven Analytics, this project transforms raw transactional data into an interactive, dynamic Excel dashboard to extract actionable business insights. The analysis focuses on understanding customer purchasing behaviors, identifying peak operational hours, and evaluating product and store performance.

## Dataset Information
* **Source:** Maven Analytics
* **Domain:** Retail / Food & Beverage
* **Data Range:** January to June 
* **Key Metrics:** Sales Revenue, Transaction Count, Product Categories, Store Locations, Time of Purchase.

## Business Questions Addressed
This analysis was a structured to answer the following core business questions:
1. How do sales vary by day of the week and hour of the day?
2. Are there any peak times for sales activity?
3. What is the total sales revenue for each month?
4. How do sales vary across different store locations?
5. What is the average price/order per person?
6. Which products are the best-selling in terms of quantity and revenue?
7. How do sales vary by product category and type?

## Dashboard & Key Insights
The interactive Excel dashboard features slicers for Month and Day, alongside critical KPIs and visualisations.

### **Core KPIs**
* **Total Sales:** $698,812.33
* **Total Footfall (Transactions):** 149,116
* **Average Order Value:** $4.69
* **Average Order Quantity:** 1.44 items

### **Key Findings**
* **Peak Traffic Hours:** Sales activity experiences a sharp incline starting at 6:00 AM, peaking significantly between **8:00 AM and 10:00 AM** (morning rush), before steadily tapering off in the afternoon.
* **Weekday Trends:** Customers volume is highly consistent throughout the workweek, with **Thursday and Friday** showing the highest order volumes (approx. 21.6K - 21.7K orders). Weekends, particularly Saturday, see a notable drop in traffic.
* **Store Performance:** Performance is highly balanced across locations. **Hell's Kitchen** leads marginally with $236k+ in sales, followed very closely by Astoria and Lower Manhattan.
* **Product Dominance:** **Coffee** is the primary revenue driver, comprising **39%** of total sales, followed by **Tea (28%)** and **Bakery items (12%)**. 
* **Top Sellers:** The **Barista Espresso** is by far the highest-grossing individual product, outperforming other top items like Brewed Chai Tea and Gourmet Brewed Coffee.
* **Size Preferences:** Large (30%) and Regular (31%) sizes make up the vast majority of specified drink orders, while Small accounts for only 9%.
